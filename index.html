Methodology & Engagement Score Definition:
Engagement Score (0–100) is calculated using a balanced multi-factor formula:
Score = (0.35 * Completion Rate) + (0.25 * Acceptance Rate) + (0.25 * Daily Volume Scale) + (0.15 * Reliability Index)

Key Insights & Opportunities
Data-Backed Takeaways
1. Driver Lifecycle Progression: Engagement declines from the New (0–30 days: 66.5) to Early-Stage (31–90 days: 58.9) tenure group, indicating a potential retention opportunity during the first 3 months.
2. Cancellation Source Focus: Passenger cancellations account for 61.2% of total cancellations, while driver cancellations account for 38.8%, highlighting where cancellation reduction efforts should focus.
3. Gold Status & Engagement: Drivers with higher Gold level counts show higher average engagement and completed rides, with Elite Gold drivers (31+) achieving a 76.4 average engagement score.
